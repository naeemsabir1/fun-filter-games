Support & Frequently Asked Questions

Need help? Find instant answers below or get in touch with our team.

1. Permissions & Hardware Access

Why does the app ask for Camera and Microphone access?

Fun Filter Games uses your front-facing selfie camera and microphone to track facial gestures (smiles, blinks, mouth opening, head tilts) and voice volume/pitch in real time. Everything is processed directly on your phone's processor using offline algorithms. No camera video or audio is ever uploaded to the internet.

What if I don't want to grant Camera permission?

You can still play! When camera permission is denied, the app automatically switches to Touch Mode. In Touch Mode, you can drag your finger to move your character or aim, and tap or hold to open mouth or activate actions.

How do I re-enable permissions if I accidentally denied them?

Open your Android device's Settings > Apps > Fun Filter Games > Permissions, and turn on Camera and Microphone. Alternatively, tap the "Fix" pill in the app to open the device settings page directly.

2. Calibration & Tracking Tips

The game isn't detecting my voice or screams properly. What should I do?

Go to Settings (gear icon in the top right or right rail) > Microphone Calibration. Follow the 2-step prompt: first stay quiet for 2 seconds so the app measures your room's ambient noise floor, then speak or shout normally. This ensures the app adapts perfectly to your room acoustics and device microphone sensitivity.

My head tilts or facial expressions feel off-center.

Hold your phone straight in front of your face at natural arm's length in good lighting. Open Settings and tap Recalibrate Neutral Face. This immediately resets the neutral baseline coordinates to your current resting expression.

3. Recording & Sharing Videos

How do I save a video of my gameplay?

Tap the big center shutter ring on the Home screen to start a recorded round. When the round ends, the Result screen will display a preview of your clip. Tap the Save button (download icon) to save the MP4 video directly into your phone's Photos/Gallery under the Movies/FunFilterGames folder.

How do I share directly to social media?

On the Result screen, you will see quick-share buttons for apps installed on your device (such as TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp, or Snapchat). Tapping any button immediately opens that app with your video clip attached. You can also tap More... to open the full Android system share sheet.

4. Fun Pass Subscriptions & Billing

What is included with Fun Pass?

Fun Pass unlocks unlimited access to all 60 games, removes the watermark from all saved and shared video clips, unlocks gold profile customization frames, and grants custom-target viral challenges.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through Google Play:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  4. Select Fun Filter Games and tap Cancel subscription.

Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current paid billing period.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

As long as you are signed into the same Google account that made the purchase, open Fun Filter Games, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Google Play will automatically sync and restore your active Fun Pass entitlement.
